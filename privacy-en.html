CropFloat Privacy Policy

Effective date

September 28, 2026. This policy describes CropFloat 0.0.1, developed by Xinyu Wang.

No data collected by CropFloat

CropFloat does not transmit captured content, personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, or diagnostic reports to the developer or third parties. It has no account system, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDKs, or app-operated server. No network entitlement is enabled.

Screen content stays on your Mac

Capture begins after you choose and authorize a window in the macOS sharing picker. ScreenCaptureKit supplies frames of that whole window; CropFloat crops those frames for display. Content outside the selected crop can therefore be present in memory. CropFloat uses the frames only to display your live preview and floating view. It does not record them to a file, upload them, inspect them with AI, or capture microphone, camera, or system audio. A camera feed displayed inside your chosen source window is treated as screen content, not direct camera access.

Local settings and retention

CropFloat saves one application preference, your selected interface language, in its local macOS sandbox. The preference is not transmitted. The source selection and crop are not saved across launches. Frames are held temporarily in memory; pausing intentionally retains the last displayed frame. Stopping capture clears the application’s displayed frame references, and quitting ends the process. CropFloat does not promise secure erasure of memory, operating-system swap, backups, or system-managed diagnostic records.

Your controls

Use Stop Capture in the CropFloat menu bar menu, or close the floating window, to request that capture stop. Closing only the crop editor leaves capture running. Quit CropFloat to end the application and its capture session. You may also use macOS sharing controls to end sharing, and manage any granted screen-recording permission in System Settings. To delete the language preference, choose Reset Language Preference in the CropFloat application menu; the interface returns to English without saving a new preference until you select a language again.

Sharing and external services

CropFloat does not sell or share data and does not perform cross-app tracking. macOS permission handling and any diagnostic sharing you enable are controlled by Apple and your system settings. The browser, camera service, or other source app you choose may have its own privacy practices; CropFloat does not control those services. If you independently contact the developer, the message and any attachments you choose to provide are separate from automatic app behavior. Please do not include sensitive screen content.

Contact and changes

Such correspondence is used to respond to your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion. Future changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy included with the app.
